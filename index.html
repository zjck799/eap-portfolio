My Best Essay

The Advancement of AI and Technology Requires People to Accept It

In this essay, I write about AI and new technology. I explain that new technology needs more than good tools. People also need to accept it.

I use the story of Barry Marshall and Robin Warren. Their research was not accepted by everyone at first. I also write about how workers feel about AI at work.

My main idea: People may not accept new ideas because they are used to old ways. But asking questions can also help us find problems and make new ideas better.

“Technology isn't the biggest challenge. Culture is.”
